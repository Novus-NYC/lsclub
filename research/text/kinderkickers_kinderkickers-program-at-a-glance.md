# kinderkickers_kinderkickers-program-at-a-glance
## 
 KinderKickers Program at a glance
## 
LSC KinderKickers is an enjoyable non-competitive program, led by professional trainers from Go2Soccer, designed to help our youngest soccer players develop basic soccer skills. These professionally designed games and activities are formatted to introduce children to fundamental soccer skills while having fun with a soccer ball. LSC KinderKickers does not teach player positioning or team tactics, as such instruction is a more advanced skill more suitable for older children. LSC KinderKickers is not a "drop off" program and parents are expected to stay and watch their children play.
Participants are assigned to teams (Pre-K and Kindergarten, by gender) and the teams meet on Saturday Mornings. For more information, please review the KinderKickers Info Guide on this site to get answers to most of your questions.
Please reach out to our KinderKickers coordinator, Carlos, at kinderkickers@lsclub.com with any questions.
