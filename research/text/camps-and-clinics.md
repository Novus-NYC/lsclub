Kickzsoccer will be running our annual Indoor Soccer Clinics for the members of the Livingston Soccer Club @the Soccer Palace, West Orange.
They will be offering a range of Skill Builder Clinics designed to develop different areas of the game, such as; Technique, Footwork, Goalscoring and Goalkeeping.
 In addition to our 5v5 Player Cup, providing players the opportunity to join a Winter League as either an individual player or with friends and teammates.
Please visit website: www.kickzsoccer.com/winter-2027
