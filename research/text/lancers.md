The top-seeded Livingston HS girls soccer team won the 2026 NJSIAA North Jersey, Section 1, Group 4 championship, defeating 3rd-seeded Ridgewood 3–2 in a shootout. This marks Livingston's second consecutive sectional title in North 1 Group 4. Congrats!
