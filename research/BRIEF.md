# Livingston Soccer Club redesign brief

Source: the club's current site, lsclub.ottosport.ai (Tahmid's "lancers" link), its PDFs and its photos.
Tahmid's notes: focus on the frontend; the backend is only sign-in for now, invite-only, on Supabase,
because the club hasn't decided what members should be able to do yet.

## Three sentences

1. Livingston Soccer Club is a volunteer-run, non-profit town club in Livingston, NJ: Saturday morning
   KinderKickers at Harrison, In-Town teams for grades 1 to 12 ending in the Carpini Cup, Travel teams
   with professional trainers, a Legends league for the dads, all played on ten town fields in green and white.
2. Visitors are Livingston parents (often on a phone at the field), the volunteer coaches and grade
   coordinators who run the teams, and travel families comparing LSC with club soccer.
3. The next step is to find your child's program and register, then check the schedule, the rules,
   the field status and who to email.

## The idea: the L is a corner of the pitch

The club's newest crest draws the letter L as the white corner marking of a soccer field, with a ball
sitting in it. So the whole site is chalked onto Livingston green: deep green full-width bands are the
turf, thin chalk-white lines are the markings (the halfway line between sections, the center circle
behind the crest, and L-shaped corner marks that frame every photo and heading), and the lettering is a
heavy slab like the crest's arched LIVINGSTON. The printed handouts parents actually use (age-group
tables, schedules, Carpini Cup results) are set on chalk-white "team sheet" bands, and the yellow of
the club's Registration button and the old shield is the one loud accent, used as a full band for the
scoreboard and the Registration buttons.

Why it is not generic: the L corner, the green and yellow and the slab lettering come from the club's
own crests; the content is the club's own (Carpini Cup, Legends team names, the ten Livingston fields,
"Kids don't care how much you know, until they know how much you care").

Photos are phone shots of mixed quality, so all of them get one treatment: a green duotone (the
current site already tints its header photo green), square corners and a chalk L corner mark.

## Palette

- Pitch green (deep, the crest's green): #12402B, the main band color
- Turf green (lighter band): #1E5C3C
- Chalk: #F5F3EA, the team-sheet bands and the type on green
- Registration yellow: #F4C430, scoreboard band and primary buttons (dark green text on it)
- Ink: #0C1F15

## Type

- Display: Alfa Slab One (heavy slab, matches the crest lettering), hero 120 to 200px on desktop
- Text: Instrument Sans (weights 400 to 700)

## Homepage, section by section

1. **Header** (pitch green): crest and name, the club's menu (In-Town, Travel, KinderKickers,
   Training Academy, Coaches Corner, Legends, Club Info), a yellow Registration button, Sign in.
2. **Hero** (pitch green, chalk lines): "Livingston" lettered across the full width, "Soccer Club"
   beneath, a chalk center circle behind. The club's own sentence about being volunteer-run. Buttons:
   Registration (yellow) and Field status. The Award Ceremony photo hangs over the halfway line.
3. **Lancers** (chalk): the girls' second straight sectional title, verbatim, beside the trophy photo,
   asymmetric 7/5.
4. **Programs** (turf green): KinderKickers, In-Town, Travel, Training Academy as four big rows with
   the ages in huge slab type and one line each from the club's own copy. No cards.
5. **Carpini Cup** (yellow): the results as a scoreboard, with the FAQ's one-line explanation of the
   cup and Phil Carpini.
6. **2026 to 2027 age groups** (chalk): the birth-date table as a team sheet.
7. **Coaches** (pitch green): "Kids don't care how much you know..." in huge type, Coach of the Year,
   volunteer to coach, link to Coaches Corner.
8. **Legends** (chalk): one huge Legends photo, past winners' team names as the one marquee.
9. **Fields** (turf green): the ten fields with map links, field status link, who closes fields.
10. **More** (chalk): headlines, store, travel vs club, partners.
11. **Footer** (pitch green): P.O. Box, board emails, menu, social, Made by Novus.

## Inner pages

- Page header: pitch green band, slab title, chalk L corner, the page's photo if it has one.
- Document lists (schedules, rules, forms) as big rows with the file type, linking to the files on the
  club's current host.
- In-Town: the division table (Schedule, Game Report, Standings, Rating Form per division).
- Sign in: invite-only. No sign-up form; the board invites people from Supabase.
