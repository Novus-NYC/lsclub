What is the Livingston Soccer Club’s KinderKickers Program?
LSC KinderKickers is an enjoyable non-competitive program, led by professional trainers from
GO2Soccer Coaching, designed to help our youngest soccer players develop basic soccer skills.
These professionally designed games and activities are formatted to introduce children to
fundamental soccer skills while having fun with a soccer ball. LSC KinderKickers does not teach
player positioning or team tactics, as such instruction is a more advanced skill more suitable for
older children. LSC KinderKickers is not a "drop off" program and parents are expected to stay
and watch their children play.
Participants are assigned to teams (Pre-K and Kindergarten, by gender) and the teams meet on
Saturday Mornings. For more information, please review the KinderKickers Info Guide on this
site to get answers to most of your questions.
Please reach out to our KinderKickers coordinator Carlos Frade
at kinderkickers@lsclub.com with any questions.
How Does the LSC KinderKickers Program Work?
SPRING SESSION
Each participant in the LSC KinderKickers program is placed on a "team" of approximately 12-16
kids. Teams will meet for one hour each week on Saturday mornings at Harrison Elementary
School weekly through the 2 time slots of 10.00-10:55 and 11:15-12:10. The teams are
separated into two age groups: Pre-K and kindergarten, by gender (all girls or all boys).
FALL SESSION
Each participant in the LSC KinderKickers program is placed on a "team" of approximately 12-16
kids. Teams will meet for one hour each week on Saturday mornings at Harrison Elementary
School rotating weekly through the 2 time slots of 9.00-10:00 and 10:30-11:30. The teams are
separated into two age groups: Pre-K and Kindergarten, by gender (all girls or all boys).
MANAGER
Each team is assigned a parent volunteer KK Team Manager who will be the liaison between the
parents and club. Team Managers will e-mail their teams with weekly practice time reminders
and other brief announcements. Managers also coordinate team matters and minor issues with
the KK Program Director. Team Managers are not expected to coach but may be asked to help
set up fields by the SDA coaches.
COACHES
Weekly session’s lesson plans have been developed by the GO2Soccer Coaching in conjunction
with the Livingston Soccer Club. These lesson plans have been thoroughly reviewed and
updated to reflect the latest coaching techniques. The lessons teach basic soccer skills, while
masquerading as children’s games. The plans are specifically designed so that each child is
constantly kicking his or her own soccer ball, having “maximum touches on the ball.”
As previously mentioned, leading these sessions will be professional soccer coaches from the
GO2Soccer Coaching . The Livingston Soccer Club has a long-standing relationship with the
GO2Soccer Coaching , utilizing their professional trainers for most of our competitive travel
soccer teams. We believe this will better prepare our players for first grade soccer and beyond.
As of 2012, LSC KinderKickers teams are no longer coached by parents or Team Managers.
Note to Team Managers: You are volunteers and parents, so please take the time to enjoy as
your own kids are on the field. You should only volunteer for this great opportunity to serve the
young kids of our community, if you are able to handle duties, solve problems with little or no
prior instruction, expeditiously coordinate with KK Program Director or the Club and stay calm in
all situations especially when involving our kids.
Who, What, Where, When and Equipment?
This year, the Fall LSC KinderKickers program begins in 5 September and runs for seven (8)
Saturdays (see dates at top of the page). There are TWO sessions each Saturday (see above for
the Spring/Fall session times). LSC KinderKickers takes place at Harrison Elementary School,
148 North Livingston Avenue. A least 2 weeks in advance, you will be contacted by a Team
Manager who will inform you about your team, schedule, contact information and field location
at the Harrison School. Every week you will report to the same field at your scheduled time.
Please do not panic if you have not heard from a KK Manager, even if you have heard from your
older child’s coach. Because our program is the only one in the Livingston Soccer Club that
continues to accept late registrations up until the season begins, we are usually still tweaking
rosters until the last minute. As a result, rosters are not sent to KK Managers until the last week
before the season start. Keep in mind that due to family vacations, it is also possible that your
team manager may be away at the time that rosters are released; if this is the case, then you
will be contacted upon their return.
At the first session of the LSC KinderKickers program, each child will receive his or her very own
official genuine LSC KinderKickers soccer ball and Team shirt.
Parents: PLEASE ... DO NOT write your child’s name on the soccer ball (it is easier to come to
the field with one ball and leave with one rather than be looking for a specific one at the end of
each session) and remember to bring it back each week. If you should lose a soccer ball, a
limited amount of replacement balls are available for $10.00 (fee payable by check to the
Livingston Soccer Club). Your child should be dressed in shorts or other weather appropriate
pants and may wear sneakers, tennis shoes or cleats. Children MUST wear shin guards to
protect them against the kicks from other players. Also, for your child’s safety, please remove all
jewelry--including earrings-- prior to each session. Additionally, each child should have an
ample supply of water based on weather conditions.
Information about Picture Day will be distributed to you by your Team Manager as soon as it is
available.
Jewelry Policy? What about My Child's New Earrings?
For safety reasons, jewelry of all types is not allowed to be worn. Please be mindful that NO
JEWELRY OF ANY KIND is to be worn on the field. This includes earrings, necklaces, bracelets,
friendship bracelets, rope bracelets, soft bracelets, metal bracelets, nose rings, or tongue
piercings. Medical bracelets are the only exception. Sweatbands are allowed, but they are not to
be used to cover up any, you guessed it, bracelets. Tape covering earrings will not be allowed.
We recognize that it is a common occurrence for children of KinderKickers age to get their ears
pierced. To avoid a potential conflict with our Jewelry Policy, please either wait until after the
soccer season ends to get your child's ears pierced, or utilize clear plastic posts as may be found
on Amazon during the game (these are specifically designed for sports – use keyword search
“Clear plastic ear post”). There is no exception to this policy. If one of the trainers or KK
Coordinators reminds you at a weekly session, keep in mind that it is not their rule, and they are
following the policy that has been made publicly available numerous times.
You had Mentioned a Team Manager--What is That?
Each team will have a Parent Team Manager volunteer or two. This person will be your contact
with the LSC KinderKickers program and, for example, will e-mail you when there is a weather
cancellation. Please address any questions to your Team Manager, who will contact you the last
week of August once the rosters are released. If you are interested in volunteering, please
contact us at KinderKickers@LSClub.com or enter that interest while registration. Team
Managers are not expected to coach. We use GO2Soccer Coaches at this age group
(professional coaches).
We need volunteers as Parent Team Managers. What are typical duties:
Pre-season start (Last week of August):
• Pick up team T-shirts and balls from the KK Program Director to bring to the field on
Session 1.
• Once you get the team roster from KK Program Director, reach out to the parents and
introduce yourself via email and make sure they know how to get the schedule,
rotations and field information.
In Season:
• Parent Team Managers have NO coaching or assist coaching responsibilities. We enroll
professional GO2Soccer Coaches– among the most well-known NJ coaching programs.
• Session 1 is a little chaotic as it’s the first-time people are finding fields etc. Gather your
team, and take them to the field location (many team managers make a poster or carry a
marked balloon or such to facilitate this on session 1)
• Be the conduit of communications between Parents and KK Program (generally one or
two mails / week)
• Maybe, establish a snack rotation for your team.
• Manage no jewelry, mandatory shin guards for your team members. Keep parents off
the fields.
• Help on Picture Day (if one is held) and the last session for distribution of trophies.
• Own it.
Parking and Specific Safety Concerns?
The parking at Harrison School is very tight. We urge you to arrive early and to carpool when
possible. The parking lot can be accessed either from North Livingston Avenue or from Ashwood
Drive. Ashwood Drive is a residential street just north of the school. Street parking is permitted
on Ashwood Drive, and the street has nice sidewalks. Since this is a residential street, we ask
that you remind your children to be respectful of the neighborhood. PLEASE, PLEASE, PLEASE do
not allow your children to play or dribble soccer balls in the parking lots, sidewalks or streets.
Please ensure that your kids hold their soccer balls while not on the field.
What is the Rainout Procedure?
If LSC KinderKickers is canceled because of inclement weather, you will be notified by your Team
Manager of that fact. Also, rainout information is available generally the morning of play, on the
Livingston Soccer Club website. LSC and the program will try, and schedule make up dates for
rained out sessions, but is not obligated to. At this age, the safety concerns are paramount and
make up dates in mid-November become unrealistic due to cold temperatures. Please note that
there are no refunds after the season starts and particularly none for rainout days.
Can I request my child to be on a team with a friend or play at a specific time?
Through our on-line registration system, you will be able to request that your child be placed on
a team with one friend. We will do our best to see that you are placed with this choice, but LSC
KinderKickers cannot guarantee the teammate request unless it is a) mutual, and b) the players
are in the same grade and gender. Putting together the team rosters is a very difficult and time-
consuming exercise. Other than for extraordinary and unforeseeable reasons, roster changes
will not be made.
Teammate requests for late registration is unlikely to be honored as most of the rosters have
been established.
As with all LSC teams and programs, game times vary from week to week. As a result, there are
no requests accepted for specific times.
