LIVINGSTON SOCCER CLUB TRAVEL PROGRAM
GUIDELINES & PROCEDURES
REGISTRATION AND COST
REGISTRATION
Before an athlete is permitted to participate on the Travel tryout process s/he must properly register with
the LSC. Registration will usually occur in April and May for the upcoming Fall and Spring seasons. The
registration process includes completing the LSC online Registration.
MEMBERSHIP FEE
As a condition of eligibility for requesting to join LSC Travel, each player must register for the tryout.
Membership fees cover League membership dues, registration fees, insurance, tournament registrations,
field costs, referee fees, and other administrative costs associated with administering and maintaining
LSC Travel. This fee is due upon acceptance to LSC Travel, and upon payment thereof becomes the
sole property of the LSC and the player and his parents/guardians waive any claim whatsoever to such
fee, regardless of whether such athlete ultimately joins LSC Travel. Travel Registration is the process
where a player is offered a spot in LSC Travel and the required forms and payment are submitted to the
LSC by the player upon acceptance of a spot in LSC Travel (“Acceptance”).
The exact amount for the coming soccer year will be listed in the Acceptance notification.
Should a player voluntarily withdraw from LSC Travel after s/he is offered to join LSC Travel and registers
at Travel Registration, the LSC Travel Membership Fee will not be refunded under any circumstances,
regardless of whether the LSC Travel Committee finds a replacement player. If LSC Travel Committee
does find a qualified replacement for the player, any unused portion of the Membership Fee will be placed
in LSC’s general fund and used by LSC for general purposes, including, but not limited to other uses of
LSC, LSC administration and charitable or scholarship donations.

TRAINING FEES
In addition to the LSC Travel Membership Fee, each athlete must pay a fee with respect to weekly
training (“Training Fees”) and a fee with respect to coaching the team on game day (“Game Day
Coaching Fees.”) The exact amount for the coming year will be listed in the Acceptance notification.
Replacement players added to a team roster after the Fall season has begun will be required to pay a
pro-rata portion of the remaining LSC Travel Membership Fee, Training Fees and Game Day Coaching
Fees.
All fees are due at Travel Registration which can be paid all at once or in 4 monthly installments.
For players added to a Travel team roster from the wait list or a subsequent tryout (i.e. a Replacement
Player), all fees are due within three (5) days of acceptance into LSC Travel and/or notification of the
amount due and are not refundable. If these funds are not paid by the above deadline, the player will not
be “carded” and may forfeit his/her roster spot on that Travel team.
As noted earlier, LSC Travel is financially self-sustaining. All costs associated with a Travel team must be
covered by that team’s players. If a team exceeds the number of coaching/training hours, the number of
tournaments (two are budgeted per team, with a maximum tournament fee established in LSC budget),
and overall games played, the team’s players will be assessed additional fees. If a player does not pay
the additional fees s/he will not be permitted to participate in LSC Travel until that fee is paid. LSC Travel
also reserves the right to take appropriate legal action if warranted. It is incumbent upon the managers
that Teams use all assigned training hours and play the minimum number of tournaments.
The LSC Travel team uniform is an additional cost. All fees are subject to change.
FINANCIAL AID
Financial assistance is limited to the cost of the LSC intown program + field fees – currently $145.
Should a player’s family have a financial hardship they should contact the LSC President or Treasurer to
request appropriate consideration. Either can be reached via email (contact information can be found at
www.lsclub.com). All information will remain private. Decisions will be made on a case-by-case basis. All
requests and the decision of the LSC will be kept strictly confidential.
REFUNDING OF COSTS
As stated earlier, a Travel team is officially formed and is deemed to be so formed by the Travel Committee
upon either the posting of the roster of such team to the LSC website, or the submission of such team roster to
the President of the LSC.
The LSC Travel Membership Fee is non-refundable. Any late registration fee is non-refundable. Should an
athlete join a Travel team mid-season (once practice has begun) or between seasons - all fees are non-
refundable upon payment. Only training payments are refundable should an athlete voluntarily separate
himself/herself from a Travel team after such team is formed but, in any event, only if the separation and
notification to the President of the LSC occurs prior to August 1 of any given year. Except as expressly
provided in these guidelines to the contrary, all fees paid to and received by the LSC are the property of the
LSC and no team or other person has any interest in or claim to such fees. Upon payment, each player and
their families expressly and unconditionally acknowledge and agree that fees are nonrefundable and waive
any claim of any kind to such fees whatsoever. Should a team player (i) move from Livingston prior to or during
a soccer seasonal year and choose to quit, or (ii) sustain an injury or illness which prevents him/her from
participating in practices and games for four (4) consecutive weeks - that player may request a pro-rata refund
for time lost which the LSC will consider, in its sole discretion whether to provide. Other than as expressly set
forth above, no other refunds will be given.
