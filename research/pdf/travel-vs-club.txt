LSC Travel vs Club Soccer
LSC Travel soccer offers a balanced, community-focused experience ideal for multi-
sport athletes, featuring lower costs ($1,250–$1,730/year) and shorter commutes. Club
soccer (premier/academy) could provide an elite, year-round pathway with college
recruiting exposure, and higher competition, though it demands significantly more time
and expense ($3,500–$15,000+/year).
Advantages of LSC Travel Soccer
• Community Connection: Travel teams are composed of local peers, building school
friendships and fostering strong family bonding due to players being from Livingston.
• Multi-Sport Friendly: Because the season is typically defined and less year-round,
players have the flexibility to participate in other sports and activities
with less scheduling conflicts.
• Financial Commitment: Costs are significantly lower than club alternatives, often
covering basic league fees, professional coaching, referee costs, and basic uniforms
rather than expensive for-profit private academy dues, out-of-state tournament
travel costs, camps, private lessons.
• Lighter Travel Burden: Games and practices are kept regional, generally requiring
drives of under an hour, making it easier for parents to manage. Higher-level
tournaments of club programs can require entire weekends and overnight hotel stays.
• Guaranteed Playing Time: Players have guaranteed playing time as per LSC
guidelines, whereas there are no guarantees at the clubs. Clubs have much larger
roster sizes, and some club players may not play in matches at all. LSC Travel players
are guaranteed a spot on the team for the year whereas club players have the constant
performance pressure of getting relegated off the team.
• Professional Coaching: LSC travel has professional coaches for all games and
practices. Many of these professionals also have coaching experience with premier
level teams.
• Select Program: LSC Travel players are eligible to join an EDP/ECNL select team in
partnership with local towns travel programs
• Red Bulls Partnership: LSC Travel players are eligible to supplement their travel
training through the Red Bulls Regional Development School and also have the
opportunity to enjoy exclusive New York Red Bulls Match Day Experiences at Sports
Illustrated Stadium
Advantages of Club (Premier) Soccer
• Pathways to Higher Play: For players targeting high school varsity, college
scholarships, or professional development, club platforms (such as the MLS NEXT,
ECNL, or GA) could provide direct scouting exposure.
• Consistent, High-Level Competition: Teams play year-round against other top-tier
academies, pushing athletes to elevate their technical speed of play and overall
competitiveness.
• Stronger Team Cohesion: With the team training together three times a week for most
of the year as well as out-of-state tournament hotel trips, players develop a deeper,
more cohesive understanding of complex team tactics and formations.
Important to note that unless the child is on the “A” team at the Club program, the
above advantages disappear. Unlike LSC, Club programs are run by for-profit
organizations and have expanded their footprint with B & C teams with large rosters that
play at the same or lower levels as LSC travel teams.
During and after the World Cup, much has been written about private equity investment
in clubs driving the costs of youth sports up and the negative impacts on our children
due to overspecialization, burnout and associated pressures thereby causing overuse
injuries and less family time. LSC is a non-profit organization with the objective of
providing a safe, professionally coached and reasonably priced soccer program for the
committed player.
