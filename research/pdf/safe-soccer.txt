Abuse Prevention Education and Training
Beginning with the 2019-2020 playing season, all adults applying for an NJYS Coach Pass
(e.g., Intown volunteer coaches, Travel Trainers, Travel Managers, Travel Director) are required
to take an initial Abuse Prevention Training course. The course is offered online through the
U.S. Center for SafeSport and integration with US Soccer Connect. Each year that the adult
volunteers or is paid to train, he or she takes a refresher course. This requirement reflects and
supports a mandate from U.S. Soccer.
Abuse comes in many different forms: physical, neglect, emotional/psychological and sexual.
Abuse manifests itself through acts of misconduct, including bullying, hazing, harassment
(including but not limited to sexual harassment), emotional misconduct, physical misconduct,
and sexual misconduct. Education and training of adults can help address and possibly thwart
one of the most preventable injuries in youth sports: child abuse. By becoming more aware and
learning how to be more engaged, we can all have an impact on abuse rates and improve the
youth soccer experience.
Managing Compliance
Beginning with the 2019/2020 seasonal year, the Livingston Soccer Club has continued to
follow U.S. Soccer mandates by requiring that our Intown recreation coaches complete a
background check and register though U.S Soccer Connect, the same program that monitors
our Travel Trainers. This program will certify all our coaches, volunteer or paid, with a criminal
history check.
Onboarding with US Soccer Connect
The Livingston Soccer Club also maintains compliance through US Soccer Connect (USSC),
the New Jersey Youth Soccer registration database, that provides a platform to manage risk for
coaches within member organizations. Also required by all our volunteer coaches, paid trainers,
volunteer managers, and Board members, are the yearly renewal of The SafeSport completion
certificate and concussion education certificate.
Conclusion: Thank you
We thank the Livingston Soccer Club community for their support of our club. It is critical that
we continue our commitment to the protection of our youth athletes. Included in our club is an
Enforcement Committee that is tasked with investigating all concerns that parents, coaches,
managers, trainers, and athletes may have so that EVERYONE can enjoy playing soccer safely.
If you see something, say something.
