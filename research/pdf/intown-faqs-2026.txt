FAQs about InTown Soccer
● How Do I register? - It’s best to use your computer rather than your
smartphone. Go to www.lsclub.com and look for the yellow button on
the top right that says “Registration”.
● How are teams formed? - In grades 1-2, teams are formed by the
club. They are also formed by the club for 7-9 Girls and HS. The other
grades will have drafts conducted by the club with the coaches for the
age group.
● May I make a friend request? - The following divisions are allowed
friend requests: 1 Girls, 1 Boys, 7-9 Girls, and Girls playing in HS. We
encourage all players to make new friends on the fields! It builds
resilience and self-esteem. They are not honored in the other grades.
● Can I switch my child’s team? - Sorry, we do not switch teams for
children once the teams are formed. It disrupts the balance and even
numbers that help make the experience more fun for everyone playing.
● When/where are practices scheduled? - The practice schedule and
location (based on field availability) are determined by the volunteer
coach once the teams are formed or drafted. This is a plus for the
volunteers who take on this important role. Coaches will be able to
sign-up for fields beginning around September 1st.
● What happens if my child cannot make the practice time or day? -
We encourage all parents to be flexible and do their best to have their
child at practice. (Remember, coaches decide the day and there are no
team switches allowed.) Coaches will not penalize a child who is not at
practice with less playing time during games.
● If I coach, may I make a request for an assistant coach? Absolutely!
We do our best to pair coaches that make mutual requests for each
other.
● May I ask for a refund once I have signed my child up? - Yes, but only
before August 1st.
● When does the season start? The weekend after Labor Day.
● When are the games played? Weekends with occasional games
during the week for grades 3-12 - see last year’s schedule as an
example https://www.lsclub.com/in-town-program
● When will I/how do I find out my team? Teams are formed after mid-
August, you will be contacted by the coach but can also see team
assignments by using the Otto Sport App that can be loaded on your
smart or android phone. Google Play or App Store
● When will I/how do I find out my coach’s name & contact
information? Check the Otto Sport App! It allows emails and chats
between players and coaches.
● Why is a late fee charged? Uniforms and equipment are ordered in
early July based on the numbers that have registered. When you
register late, we pay more for extra items to outfit your athlete. Only
one late fee is charged per family and waivers are offered for new
residents.
● Who can I contact if I have a question? “Ask three then me,” applies
here too! First, read these FAQs, then your coach, then the Grade
Coordinator. If this still doesn’t help ask the then head of InTown. The
last person to email is the president. List of Contacts
● When is the latest I can register - If space allows on the team, you
may register right up to the start of the season in September. Some
grades close earlier than that and you may be put on a waitlist.
● What is the Carpini Cup? Season ending tournament named after the
founder of our club Phil Carpini for grades 3-12
● What are the requirements to be a coach? All coaches complete a
Background Check and Safety Training. This protects our players and
our coaches. We encourage parents, relatives, and even High School
students to volunteer!
● What are the responsibilities of a head coach vs assistant coach?
Assistant coach helps the head coach with running a weekly practice
and game day coaching
● Can I play Intown and Travel soccer? Travel and Club players are
encouraged to join the fun of Intown.
● Who decides if fields are closed? Town of Livingston makes the
decision based on weather/field conditions
● Are there any programs outside of the Fall season? Yes, there are
clinics and camps that we will send out information about throughout
the year
