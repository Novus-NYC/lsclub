# Livingston Soccer Club: press and public-source research

Compiled 2026-11-25. This file only lists facts that are not on the club site. Every item gives a source URL and a short verbatim quote. Dates are the publication date where one was available; "inferred" means the date was worked out from the article text. Items marked **UNCERTAIN** need a check with the club before they go on the site.

Note on access: TAPinto pages return HTTP 403 to the fetch tool, so their article text was read from the page's embedded data. The West Essex Tribune now redirects to newjerseyhills.com, which could not be read. Its Delman obituary is covered below through Dignity Memorial instead.

## 1. People behind the club (best material for an "Our story" page)

### Steven Delman: founding-era board member, 40+ years on the board (died Jan 12, 2024)
- Source: Dignity Memorial obituary (Jan 2024). https://www.dignitymemorial.com/obituaries/livingston-nj/steven-delman-11624395
  - "over 40 years of service on the Board of the Livingston Soccer Club."
  - "As a coach, he touched the lives of thousands of children"
  - He helped build the Soccer Circle at Friendship Circle: "played a crucial role in developing the soccer circle"
- The same obituary appears in the Star-Ledger on Legacy.com: https://www.legacy.com/us/obituaries/starledger/name/steven-delman-obituary?id=54106652
- A West Essex Tribune item, "In Loving Memory of Steven Delman," exists but could not be read (the site redirects): https://www.westessextribune.net/article/3908,in-loving-memory-of-steven-delman
- He was a former club president. TAPinto, board dinner article (c. March 2010; see section 3): "Former club president Steve Delman presented the Delman Coaches Sportsmanship Award"
- **UNCERTAIN:** A MapQuest listing (https://www.mapquest.com/us/new-jersey/livingston-soccer-club-inc-423910993) reportedly calls him a "founding board member." Search snippets showed this, but the page came back empty when fetched. Treat it as unverified.
- **Website use:** This explains the name of the **Delman** award. It honors Steve Delman, a former president, longtime coach and 40-year board member. Per the 2010 article, the award is the "Delman Coaches Sportsmanship Award," not a scholarship. The club may call it the "Steve Delman Coaches Sportsmanship Award" (see section 3).

### Phil Carpini: the scholarships are a memorial
- Source: LSC's 2022 scholarship instructions PDF, hosted on the club's own file server (a club document, not press, but not on the current site). https://cdn2.ottosport.ai/_deimos/_public_files/0hwqf1qkywyn8/2022InstructionsCarpiniScholarshipApplication%20(4)%20(2).pdf?CacheKey=1647556108
  - "Each year the Livingston Soccer Club (LSC) awards Phil Carpini Memorial college scholarships"
  - "The total value of the scholarship pool is $10,000" (2022)
  - "The maximum value of each individual award is $2,000"
  - Players and referees are both eligible: "associated with LSC as a player and/or a referee"
  - In 2022 the Scholarship Committee chair was Harold Hornstein.
- **Not found:** No obituary, tribute or press profile of Phil Carpini turned up in searches of Legacy.com, Dignity Memorial, TAPinto, Patch or the West Essex Tribune, so there is no founding year or biography for him. Ask the club.

### Erin Donnelly Scholarship
- **Not found** in any press or public source. Searches only returned unrelated people. Ask the club who it honors. Do not guess.

### Other long-serving volunteers named in the press
- Andy Feigenberg, from TAPinto's board dinner article (c. March 2010): "Feigenberg, who has served on the Board for 13 years, steps down as president after six years leading the club." He is still a trustee in the club's FYE2025 IRS filing (ProPublica, below).

## 2. Club scale and finances (public IRS data)

- Source: ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, EIN 22-3500453. https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/223500453
  - Fiscal year ends in February. Total revenue was **$543,761 (FY2025)**, $587,775 (FY2024) and $581,833 (FY2023). Total assets were $1,064,961 in FY2025.
  - FY2021 (the COVID year) shows revenue of -$100,663, which suggests refunds.
- Source: GuideStar profile. https://www.guidestar.org/profile/22-3500453
  - Mission: "TO FOSTER, PROMOTE, ENCOURAGE, AND SUPPORT THE PLAYING OF SOCCER BY THE YOUTH OF LIVINGSTON"
  - IRS ruling year 1998. This is the tax-exemption date, not a founding date.
- **Website use:** Do not print the dollar figures. They are useful background only (for example, "a volunteer-run non-profit with a budget of about half a million dollars").

### Player counts (only where a source states them)
- **2,500 registered players** (the 2009 season). TAPinto, c. March 2010: "Last season the LSC had 2500 registered players" https://www.tapinto.net/articles/livingston-soccer-club-holds-annual-board-dinner
- **About 1,600 in-town players** (same article): "the 1600 or so kids who played in the Intown Program"
- **2,400 soccer participants in town in 2005** (all organizations, not only LSC). Livingston Master Plan, Section VIII, Recreation & Parks (c. 2007–08): "soccer 2,400". This was the largest of all the sports listed, ahead of softball at 1,700 and baseball at 1,250. https://livingstonnj.org/DocumentCenter/View/687/Section-VIII---Recreation-and-Parks-Plan-Element-PDF?bidId=
- **Club age:** "Over 40 years" appears only in the unverified MapQuest snippet. The strongest public statement is Delman's "over 40 years" on the board, from his January 2024 obituary. That puts the club's start at about **1983 or earlier** (inference; **UNCERTAIN**).

## 3. Traditions, awards and the annual dinner

Source for everything in this section: TAPinto, "Livingston Soccer Club Holds Annual Board Dinner and Awards Ceremony." It is undated on the page. The dinner was "Wednesday evening, March 3rd" and the article mentions "Fall 2010" registration, which puts it in **March 2010** (inferred). https://www.tapinto.net/articles/livingston-soccer-club-holds-annual-board-dinner

- The annual board dinner and awards were held at Nero's Restaurant.
- The club ran its own awards, several of them long-standing:
  - **Harold Shatz Referee Award:** "The twelfth annual Harold Shatz Referee Award". It was given to teenage referees, here two LHS tenth graders, and dates back to about 1998.
  - **Delman Coaches Sportsmanship Award:** "cited for good sportsmanship, fairness, and the ability to make the game exciting"
  - **Kinderkickers Coaches Award:** the fourth annual one in 2010. Quote: "enthusiasm and silliness are the best inspiration at the Kinderkickers level"
  - **Silbermann Community Service Award:** the fourth annual one, presented by past president Max Silbermann.
- At the same dinner, the LHS varsity coaches honored the 2009 NJ All-State players.
- Fee and website facts from 2010: "We have maintained our $60 registration fee, introduced online registration"
- **Coach of the Year (later years):** The old lsclub.com has a page "Meet Jody Hill - Our Coach of the Year 2020." http://www.lsclub.com/meet-jody-hill-our-coach-of-the-year-2020. This is the club's own older site. The search snippet linked it to the "Steve Delman Coaches Sportsmanship Award." **UNCERTAIN:** not opened and verified.

### Soccer Circle (play for children with special needs, run with Friendship Circle)
- TAPinto (2010): "Soccer Circle, which provides a day of soccer activities for special needs children in conjunction with the Friendship Circle." It also said JoAnn Lee "has attended 26 of the 28 Soccer Circle events."
- Montgomery Academy blog (2014), which says it started as a family B'nai Mitzvah project of the Feigenbergs: "as a one-time trial for their children Bobby and Staci's B'nai Mitzvah project" http://www.montgomeryacademyonline.org/blog/special-needs-soccer/
  - "the 42nd soccer circle program celebrated its 12th anniversary on Father's Day" (2014, which implies it started about 2002).
- Soccer Circle Facebook page: "teenage volunteers act as one-one-one trainers" https://www.facebook.com/SoccerCircle/
- The Carpini scholarship criteria list "Soccer Circle volunteer" as counting toward eligibility (2022 PDF, section 1).
- **Website use:** This is a strong community story. It is volunteer-built, teen-powered, and linked to the scholarship.

## 4. Carpini Cup (in-town season finale)

- **Live draw on local TV.** Livingston Township, LTV Sports page: https://www.livingstonnj.org/511/LTV-Sports
  - "Be sure to check out the LIVE Carpini Cup Selection Show each year in October."
  - "a live lottery-style drawing of ping-pong balls to determine the match-ups"
  - "Teams are selected and the grids are posted."
  - LTV lists LSC "(including the annual "Carpini Cup Selection Show")" among its featured programs.
- **Scale in 2011.** Patch, Nov 19, 2011: https://patch.com/new-jersey/livingston/livingston-soccer-tournament
  - "the Carpini Cup will feature 79 teams in nine different tournament[s]"
  - Brackets: "third-, fourth-, fifth-sixth and seventh-ninth grade brackets will be held for both boys and girls," and "the 10th-12thgrade tourney will be co-ed."
  - Semifinals and finals were at Heritage Middle School.
  - "slated to be played Halloween weekend but was cancelled after a freak snowstorm" (the October 2011 snowstorm).
  - The 2011 edition was a fundraiser for the Make Some Noise: Cure Kids Cancer Foundation, "benefitting pediatric cancer research."
  - Neil Grossman was "in his ninth year as chairman of the Carpini Cup Tournament Committee." That means he had chaired it since about 2003.
- **Recent dates** (club schedule PDFs): Carpini Cup Oct 25/26, 2025 (rain dates Nov 1/2), and Oct 26/27, 2024. https://lsclub.demosphere-secure.com/_files/in-town-program/2025%20IT%20Sched%20v4hs.pdf
- **Past in-town team names:** BallCharts team pages show in-town teams named after national teams (Brasil, France, Mexico, Greece, Holland). Search snippets said "Greece and Holland were named co-champions" of the 2009 Cup. **UNCERTAIN:** these are user-made team pages and were not opened. http://www.ballcharts.com/teams/index.php?team=lsbrazil

## 5. Fields and places (local color for a "Where we play" page)

### Madonna Drive turf: the Saint Barnabas Medical Center (SBMC) Community Field (opened 2017)
- TAPinto, "Livingston Christens New Saint Barnabas Medical Center Community Field" (2017; Mayor Shawn Klein). https://www.tapinto.net/towns/livingston/sections/business-and-finance/articles/livingston-christens-new-saint-barnabas-medical-c
  - "officially unveiled as the Saint Barnabas Medical Center Community Field"
  - "Thanks to the $1.3 million donation from SBMC"
  - "a vision that was nearly 10 years in the making"
  - Mayor Klein: "the biggest complaint that they had was the fields"
- TAPinto, Madonna Field open forum (undated, c. 2016–17): https://www.tapinto.net/towns/livingston/sections/government/articles/livingston-township-council-holds-open-forum-on-m
  - The land came from St. Philomena's in 2008: "purchased this particular property from St. Philomena's Church in 2008 for $1.2 million"
  - It was planned with "four 70-foot light posts" by Musco.
  - LHS AD David Cohen: "This field is a game changer."
- First use reported: TAPinto, the July 4, 2017 cricket exhibition: "The new Madonna Turf Field was put to use on the 4th of July" https://www.tapinto.net/towns/livingston/sections/sports/articles/july-4th-celebration-in-livingston-features-first
- Address: Northern Counties Soccer Association field directions give 400 Madonna Drive, behind the high school. https://www.ncsanj.com/fieldDirections.cfm?fid=908

### Okner Athletic Field Complex
- Livingston Master Plan, Section VIII (c. 2007–08), same PDF as section 2: "The new, 12 acre, Okner complex was dedicated in October of 2006." It "adds ... 2 soccer fields."
- **Turf conversion proposed in 2024.** Patch, Feb 28, 2024: https://patch.com/new-jersey/livingston/livingston-seeks-funding-turf-field-bathroom-community-center. The council backed an NJ DCA grant application of about $1.5 million "replacing existing natural grass fields with turf field."
- The grant application itself is on NJ Legislature budget documents: https://pub.njleg.state.nj.us/publications/budget/DCA%20sharepoint/livingston%20okner%20complex%20application%202024-09104-0379.pdf
  - Total cost $2,500,000: $1.5M requested from the state plus $1.0M from the Township Open Space Trust Fund.
  - "This project will provide a second turf field"
  - "natural grass fields cannot handle use and have to be shut down to recover"
  - "youth sports programs involve thousands of participants"
  - **UNCERTAIN:** No source confirmed whether the grant was awarded or the turf built. A fall 2025 adult league still listed Okner as grass.

### Fields before Okner (history)
- In the same Master Plan table (pre-2006), LSC is listed among the soccer users of 2 fields at Memorial Oval, 4 at Heritage Middle School, 4 at Mt. Pleasant, and others. The plan's assessment: "the fields have no time for recovery or replanting of grass seed."

## 6. Programs and moments covered by the press (club history timeline)

- **2012: Kinderkickers goes professional.** Patch, Apr 18, 2012. https://patch.com/new-jersey/livingston/changes-to-the-kinderkickers-coming-this-year
  - "the club will be staffed full-time by professional soccer trainers from the Sports Domain Academy." Before this, parents coached.
  - President Steve Toby: "better prepare our wee ones for first grade soccer and beyond"
  - The 2012 cost was $65 per child, including a ball and shirt. There were seven Saturday sessions.
  - Note: the club's current KinderKickers partner is Go2Soccer (per the club). The club's 2023 KK guidelines PDF also mentions a "long-standing relationship" with Sports Domain Academy. https://lsclub.demosphere-secure.com/_files/kinderkickers/2023%20KK%20Program%20Guidelines%20(1).pdf (search snippet; not opened)
- **2020: the COVID response.** TAPinto, Mar 19, 2020. https://www.tapinto.net/towns/livingston/sections/coronavirus-news/articles/livingston-soccer-club-launches-virtual-training-program-and-fifa-20-tournament
  - "a free, virtual training program for players of all ages"
  - It added a town-wide FIFA 20 tournament for Livingston Neighbors Helping Neighbors, with a suggested $5 donation.
  - Board member Steve Brooks: "In all honesty, we're bored," and "Normally, we are volunteering our time on soccer fields"
  - It included "Multiple Zoom sessions with professional trainers every day"
- **2011:** The registration deadline for fall in-town (grades 1 to 12) was May 1. Patch: https://patch.com/new-jersey/livingston/last-chance-livingston-soccer-club-signup-ends-today

## 7. Livingston High School Lancers soccer (alumni pathway / community pride)

### Girls: 2025 state champions, 2026 sectional champions again
- **2025: first Group 4 state title in program history.** TAPinto, 2026 season preview (published about Sept 2026): https://www.tapinto.net/towns/livingston/sections/sports/articles/livingston-girls-soccer-to-kick-off-2026-season-as-defending-state-champs-under-new-coach
  - "winning the Group 4 state championship for the first time in program history last year"
  - The 2025 record was 20-3-1. They beat Westfield 1-0 in the semifinal and Cherokee 2-1 in the final, in coach Brian Carr's last game.
  - The new 2026 coach is Oscar Viteri, an LHS Class of 1985 graduate whose career record was "383-160-31" (Mount St. Dominic and Glen Ridge).
  - College commits named: Daisy Cheng (VMI), Ayla Sherman (William Smith), Olivia Hans (Emory).
- **2026 sectional.** TAPinto (Nov 2026): https://www.tapinto.net/towns/livingston/sections/sports/articles/livingston-girls-soccer-beats-ridgewood-3-2-in-shootout-to-win-sectional-title
  - "won its second consecutive NJSIAA North 1, Group 4 championship"
  - Livingston was the top seed at 19-1-1. They came back from 2-0 down at halftime and won the shootout 4-2 after two overtimes.
  - Emily Fernandes scored and assisted, Olivia Hans scored, Ayla Sherman made 8 saves, and "Arielle Stein had the decisive penalty kick."
  - MaxPreps confirms the date as Nov 13, 2026: https://www.maxpreps.com/news/jRydaqVcPk-Ycn3R2pa9wg/soccer-recap-livingston-is-victorious.htm
- **2026 state semifinal: lost 5-1 at undefeated Westfield.** TAPinto (Nov 2026): https://www.tapinto.net/towns/livingston/sections/sports/articles/girls-soccer-livingston-falls-one-game-short-of-state-finals-losing-to-westfield-in-group-4-semifinal
  - Livingston led 1-0 at the half on a goal by sophomore Stella DiFrancisco, and finished the season 19-2-1. Sherman made 14 saves.
  - Viteri: "We played with a lot of heart in the first half"
- **2024 season:** the girls won the SEC American Division and the Essex County Championship. TAPinto, Big L Club Night of Champions (2025): https://www.tapinto.net/towns/livingston/sections/giving-back/articles/livingston-s-big-l-club-names-2025-athletes-of-the-year-honors-scholarship-winners-and-championship-title-holders
  - That article also says keeper Antonia Giordano (Class of 2025) "led the No. 1 defense in New Jersey."

### Boys
- Coach Roger Rubinetti has had a very long tenure. He was already boys varsity coach in 2010 (TAPinto board dinner) and is still listed on the LHS fall staff page: https://lhs.livingston.org/athletics/coaching-staff/fall-coaching-staff
- 2026: the regular-season record was mixed. MaxPreps' mid-October snapshot showed 6-7-4, including a 3-0 win over Montgomery on Oct 3, 2026. https://www.maxpreps.com/nj/livingston/livingston-lancers/soccer/schedule/
