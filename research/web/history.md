partner/third-party pages. The current OttoSport site (lsclub.ottosport.ai) was NOT re-scraped.

Rules followed: every fact has a source URL and a short quote (under 15 words). Archive URLs carry
the capture timestamp (YYYYMMDDhhmmss), so the date of each page is in the URL. Where nothing was
found, that is said plainly. Names of private individuals (scholarship winners, players, Geezers
rosters) are deliberately left out even where the old site printed them.

Abbreviations: WB = `https://web.archive.org/web/<timestamp>/<url>`.

- The spirit, in the club's words (2025 handout, live site): the Cup "has always been viewed as a 'Second Chance' tournament" and "Anything Can Happen" (these two phrases sit in an oddly encoded font in the PDF; decoded by hand) (https://www.lsclub.com/_files/2025%20Carpini%20Handout.pdf). Strong, real copy for the site.

### Carpini Cup rules (2019 handout, condensed, all from https://web.archive.org/web/20201125103322/http://www.lsclub.com/_files/carpini-cup/Carpini%202019%20handout.pdf)
- Groups of 3 to 5 teams seeded by season results, then a random draw for Saturday opponents.
- Saturday: each team plays two group games; top two per group go to Sunday semifinals/finals.
- Game length: 3rd/4th grade girls and 3rd grade boys "four 11 minute quarters"; older grades two 25-minute halves (7th to 9th boys two 30-minute halves on Fri/Sat).
- Scoring: "7 points for each win", "3 points for each tie", "1 point per goal up to a maximum of 3 points per game", "1 point for a shutout". Max 11 points per game.
- Semifinals: 1st v 4th, 2nd v 3rd; must be played out. Grades 3 to 6: 5 minutes sudden-death 6v6 (7v7), then 5 minutes 5v5 (6v6) "with no goalie", then FIFA penalty kicks. Grades 7 to 12 go straight to kicks.
- Finals: two 5-minute sudden-death overtime periods; if still tied "a draw shall occur and co-champions declared."
- Shootout: "A MEMBER OF THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES WILL BE PRESENT THROUGHOUT THE SHOOT-OUT".
- Conduct: "At the end of the game, teams must shake hands off the field." "All Livingston Soccer Club playing rules (including playing time!) apply."

## 4. Other programs the current site doesn't tell the story of

### Soccer Circle (special-needs program, LSC co-sponsored)
- 2004: "started about 2 years ago as the B'nai Mitzvah project" of two LSC kids; "Co-sponsored by the Livingston Soccer Club, Ashley's Soccer Camp, and the Friendship Circle"; "Our teenage volunteer 'trainers' are the heart and soul of this program" and most are "current or former players from our own Livingston Soccer Club" teams (https://web.archive.org/web/20040610115531/http://www.lsclub.com:80/soccercircle.htm).
- 2016: "Soccer Circle 47", "approaching the 14th anniversary of Soccer Circle", at "Livingston HS Turf Field"; "a much needed sports program for the children with special needs" (https://web.archive.org/web/20160824024019/http://www.lsclub.com:80/docs/SoccerCircle47.pdf). 2016 homepage: "Soccer Circle promotes a greater awareness and understanding of people with special needs" (WB 20170322080205 homepage). Whether it still runs: unknown.

### KinderKickers (original 2000 description, still the program's DNA)
- "an enjoyable non-competitive program designed to help our youngest of soccer players"; "emphasizes frequent contact with a soccer ball"; parent volunteers "are not 'coaches'"; "Enthusiasm, energy, liberal use of encouraging words and a friendly smile are the chief requirements." Held at Harrison Elementary School, seven Saturday mornings; trainers from Ashley's Soccer Camp (https://web.archive.org/web/20010205161800/http://lsclub.com:80/kinder.htm, last changed Dec 2000).
- 2003 PDF: lessons "teach a basic soccer skill while masquerading as a child's game"; "Do not be disappointed if all the kids beehive around the ball." "your future Pele or Mia Hamm" (https://web.archive.org/web/20030917121503/http://www.lsclub.com:80/AllAboutKinderKickers.pdf). Lovely parent-facing copy.
- Long-running tagline 2011 to 2017: "KINDERKICKER PROGRAM makes the player's experience with soccer so enjoyable" that kids choose soccer on their own (WB 20110131153954 homepage).

### In-Town playing-time rules (a club value worth showing)
- 1st/2nd grade rotation "assures that each child has a chance to learn ALL of the positions"; no standings for 1st/2nd grade; "Every child must play at least one-half of each game"; 3rd to 6th grade "at least one-quarter of each game in a position other than Defender ... or Goalkeeper"; an Enforcement Committee; spectators behind a "set-back" line (https://web.archive.org/web/20091013081408/http://www.lsclub.com:80/intownprogram/328281.html; same rules in 2003 letter).
